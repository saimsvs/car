# Privacy Policy

**CarTrack**

**Operated by:** ForgeTech ("Service Provider," "we," "us," or "our")

**Effective date:** September 8, 2026  
**Last updated:** September 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and share information when you use the CarTrack mobile application (the "Application").

By downloading, installing, or using the Application, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect / Store

### 1.1 Information you provide (stored locally)

When you use the Application, you may enter:

- Vehicle details (name, year, make, model, trim, plate, mileage)  
- Fuel, service, expense, and income logs  
- Reminders and notes  
- App preferences (currency, units, reminder settings)

**This data is stored on your device.** CarTrack is a **local-first** application and does not require creating an account for core features.

### 1.2 Backup via Google Drive (optional)

If you use **Backup & export**, the Application creates a backup file (for example JSON) on your device and opens the system share sheet. You may choose **Google Drive** (or another app) to store that file.

- We do **not** receive your Google password.  
- We do **not** access your full Google Drive.  
- Any copy saved to Google Drive is controlled by you and subject to [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### 1.3 Information collected automatically

Limited technical information may be processed by the OS or app stores for installs and crash diagnostics. We do not use third-party advertising analytics SDKs for targeted ads in the local-first Application.

### 1.4 Information we do not collect

We do not intentionally collect:

- Precise GPS location for core logging  
- Contacts  
- Microphone or camera recordings for core features  
- SMS or email content from your device  

---

## 2. How We Use Information

Local data is used only to:

- Provide vehicle tracking, reminders, and reports on your device  
- Export a backup file when you request it  

---

## 3. Sharing

We do **not** sell your personal information.

Data leaves your device only when **you** share or back up a file using your device’s share sheet or another service you choose.

---

## 4. Data Retention and Deletion

- Local data remains until you delete entries, clear app data, or uninstall the Application.  
- Backup files in Google Drive remain until you delete them from Drive.

---

## 5. Children’s Privacy

The Application is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

---

## 6. Security

We rely on your device’s storage protections. No method of electronic storage is 100% secure. You are responsible for securing backups you save to Google Drive or other locations.

---

## 7. Changes

We may update this Policy from time to time. The effective / last updated dates will change when we do.
