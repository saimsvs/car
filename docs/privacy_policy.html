CarTrack Privacy Policy

ForgeTech
Effective Date: September 8, 2026 | Last Updated: September 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how ForgeTech handles information when you use the CarTrack mobile application ("Application").

CarTrack is local-first. Vehicle logs, expenses, reminders, and preferences are stored on your device. Optional backup lets you export a file you can save to Google Drive or another location you choose.

1. Information stored on your device

You may enter vehicle details, mileage, fuel/service/expense/income logs, reminders, notes, and preferences. This data stays on your device. An account is not required for core features.

2. Optional Google Drive backup

Backup & export creates a file and opens your device share sheet. You may choose Google Drive. We do not receive your Google password or access your full Drive. Copies in Drive are governed by Google’s Privacy Policy.

3. What we do not collect

We do not intentionally collect precise GPS for core logging, contacts, mic/camera recordings for core features, or SMS/email content from your device.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data leaves your device only when you explicitly share or back up a file.

5. Deletion

Delete data in the app, clear app storage, or uninstall. Delete Drive backups from your Google account.

6. Children

Not directed to children under 13.
